# Which tool do I need?

Three independent tools for single-cell multimodal data. They don't share a data
format or API. Pick the one that matches your goal, then open its Methods page
for the details.

| Your goal | Use | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Integrate** multimodal data across conditions or patients, and trace results back to genes, cells, and cell types | **M3** | Condition-aware latent factors, patient-level predictions, gene, cell, and cell-type attributions |
| **Compare & choose** an integration method, from our benchmark or on your own data | **scMultiBench** | 40+ methods scored with scIB metrics, rankings & bubble plots, reproduce them on your own data |
| **Train one model for many tasks** on multimodal data, classify cells, reduce dimensions, select markers, simulate cells | **Matilda** | Cell-type predictions, embeddings, marker genes, synthetic cells |
